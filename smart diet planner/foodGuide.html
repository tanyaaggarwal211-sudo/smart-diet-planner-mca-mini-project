Explore healthy foods

Simple ingredients for balanced meals.

6 food ideas
🍎 FRUIT
Vitamins Fiber

Apple

A convenient fruit that provides fiber and a naturally sweet snack.

Try it: Add slices to breakfast or enjoy as a snack.
🥚 PROTEIN
Protein Vitamin B12

Eggs

Provide protein and nutrients that support normal body functions.

Try it: Boiled eggs with whole-grain toast.
🌾 WHOLE GRAIN
Fiber Energy

Oats

A whole grain that supplies carbohydrates and soluble fiber.

Try it: Make porridge with milk and fruit.
🥛 DAIRY
Calcium Protein

Milk or curd

Can provide protein and calcium; choose options that suit your diet.

Try it: Include plain curd with a meal.
🥬 VEGETABLE
Fiber Micronutrients

Leafy greens

Vegetables add color, fiber, and a variety of vitamins and minerals.

Try it: Add spinach to dal, sabzi, or a wrap.
🫘 PLANT PROTEIN
Protein Fiber

Dal and beans

Affordable plant foods that provide protein, fiber, and minerals.

Try it: Pair dal with rice or roti and vegetables.
🌿

A simple healthy-eating tip

Try to include vegetables or fruit, a protein source, and a grain in your meals.
